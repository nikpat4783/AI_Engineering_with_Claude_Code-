What is ALCOA+?

ALCOA+ is a data governance and quality framework essential for maintaining data integrity in regulated environments. It ensures that all data collected, recorded, and used in operations meets strict compliance standards required by FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and other regulatory bodies.

Purpose: To establish a consistent approach to data management that demonstrates the reliability, authenticity, and trustworthiness of data throughout its lifecycle.

Scope: Applies to all data elements in operational systems, laboratories, manufacturing processes, and quality control activities.

A
Attributable
Every data entry must be traceable to the individual who created or modified it, with clear timestamps and identification.

Key Requirements:

  • User identification and authentication for all system access
  • Electronic signatures or audit trails capturing who, what, when, and why
  • Unique user identifiers that cannot be shared
  • Timestamp synchronization across all systems

Attributable Checklist:

L
Legible
All data must be clearly written, displayed, and permanently recorded in a format that is easily readable and understandable.

Key Requirements:

  • Data must be clear and not obscured or illegible
  • Standardized units and terminology used consistently
  • No use of abbreviations that could be misinterpreted
  • Permanent ink or electronic records that cannot fade
  • Original documents preserved in original format

Legibility Checklist:

C
Contemporaneous
Data must be recorded at the time the activity occurs, not before or after the event takes place.

Key Requirements:

  • Real-time data entry as activities are performed
  • No retroactive entry of data after the fact
  • Timestamps that accurately reflect when data was created
  • System locks that prevent premature data entry
  • Backups and disaster recovery procedures in place

Contemporaneous Checklist:

O
Original
The original data must be preserved with all amendments and modifications documented as separate records, never overwritten.

Key Requirements:

  • Original data never deleted or overwritten
  • All corrections recorded with reason and authorization
  • Amendment trails showing all changes made
  • Previous values always visible in audit trail
  • Proper change control procedures in place

Original Data Checklist:

A
Accurate
Data must be precise, correct, and free from errors. All measurements must be validated and calibrated according to standards.

Key Requirements:

  • Instrument calibration and validation procedures
  • Data validation and verification before recording
  • Standard operating procedures (SOPs) followed consistently
  • Training and qualification of personnel
  • Quality control checks and peer review

Accuracy Checklist:

+
Complete (Plus)
All required data fields must be filled without gaps, omissions, or missing information that could affect the record's integrity.

Key Requirements:

  • All mandatory fields completed before record closure
  • No empty or null fields where data should exist
  • Clear notation if data is not applicable (N/A)
  • Reason documented if field is intentionally left blank
  • Records cannot be finalized with missing data

Completeness Checklist:

+
Consistent (Plus)
Data must be recorded using standardized methods, formats, and terminology across all operations and systems.

Key Requirements:

  • Standardized data entry formats and protocols
  • Consistent naming conventions and terminology
  • Uniform SOPs across all departments
  • Regular training on standardized procedures
  • System validation for format compliance

Consistency Checklist:

+
Enduring (Plus)
Records must be durable and maintained throughout their required retention period without deterioration or loss.

Key Requirements:

  • Data storage in stable, non-degrading media
  • Backup and disaster recovery procedures
  • Regular integrity checks and maintenance
  • Retention policies aligned with regulatory requirements
  • Archive procedures that preserve accessibility

Endurance Checklist:

+
Available (Plus)
Records must be easily accessible for review, inspection, and regulatory submission throughout their required retention period.

Key Requirements:

  • Accessible storage systems and retrieval procedures
  • Proper indexing and documentation for easy location
  • Controlled access with audit trails
  • Regular testing of data accessibility
  • Migration procedures for format changes

📋 Regulatory Compliance & Standards

FDA 21 CFR Part 11: Establishes requirements for electronic records and signatures in regulatory submissions.
ICH Q14: Guidelines on analytical procedure development and lifecycle management.
GAMP 5: Good Automated Manufacturing Practice guidelines for validated systems.
ISO/IEC 27001: Information security management system standards.
EU GMP Annex 11: European guidelines for computerized systems in pharmaceutical operations.

🎯 Key Implementation Takeaways

ALCOA+ is not just about data quality—it's about demonstrating that data can be trusted for regulatory decision-making
Implementation requires commitment from all levels: leadership, operations, IT, and quality teams
Regular training and awareness programs are essential for maintaining compliance culture
System validation and periodic audits ensure ongoing compliance with ALCOA+ principles
Documentation of procedures and evidence of compliance is critical for regulatory inspections
Each principle supports the others—they work together as an integrated system
